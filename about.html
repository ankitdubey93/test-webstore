Our story

Small by design, since 2015.

Lumière was founded in 2015 by Camille Rousseau, a master colourist with more than twenty years behind the chair. After years in large commercial salons, Camille wanted somewhere calmer — a place with a short service menu, unhurried appointments, and enough time to actually talk with the person in the chair before touching their hair.

That's still how we work. We keep the team small, book generous time slots, and include a complimentary consultation before every colour service. We'd rather do a handful of things exceptionally well than offer everything under one roof — which is why you won't find nail services here.

Our studio sits on Maple Street in the Pearl District, with a ground-floor, wheelchair accessible entrance and a streetcar stop two minutes away.
