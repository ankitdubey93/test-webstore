Good to know

Our policies, in plain language.

Cancellations

Free up to 24 hours before your appointment. Within 24 hours we charge 50% of the service price, and no-shows are charged the full price. If you're more than 15 minutes late we may need to reschedule you.

Colour deposits

Colour and balayage bookings require a $50 deposit at the time of booking. It's credited toward your final bill, and every colour service includes a free consultation first.

Payment

We accept Visa, Mastercard, debit cards, cash and Apple Pay. We do not accept American Express. Gratuity is appreciated but never required.

Parking & access

Street parking on Maple Street, plus a paid public garage one block north on 10th Avenue. The salon is wheelchair accessible with a ground-floor entrance, and the NW 10th & Marshall streetcar stop is a 2-minute walk away.
